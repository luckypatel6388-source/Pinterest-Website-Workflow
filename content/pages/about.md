Strand Notes was founded to cut through the marketing noise surrounding modern hair tools and care routines. Whether you are navigating thermal damage, searching for the right plate architecture, or trying to find a styler that actually works for your specific strand density, we break down the mechanics so you can buy with total confidence.

We design clear, editorial-driven guides specifically tailored for everyday styling—focusing on what a tool actually does, who it is engineered for, and exactly who should skip it.

## How We Select & Review Products

- We read specs, brand documentation and verified buyer reviews.
- We say so when we have not tested a product ourselves.
- Nobody pays for a place in a guide.
- We show no prices, because they change daily.
- We make no medical or hair-growth claims.
